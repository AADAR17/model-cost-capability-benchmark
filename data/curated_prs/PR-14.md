## Description
Fixes undefined behavior in BITFIELD's signed-overflow check: the lower increment bound for signed 64-bit operations was computed with a subtraction that could itself overflow, triggering UBSan (Undefined Behavior Sanitizer) detection. Fix casts to unsigned arithmetic before the subtraction.

