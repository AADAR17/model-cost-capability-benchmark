## Description
Optimizes Redis hash operations by batching template mutations: instead of switching templates once per field during HSET/HDEL/HGETDEL/HSETEX with multiple fields, all fields are collected first and a single template switch is performed per command, instead of one template switch per field. Reported improvement: ~2.7x-6x throughput depending on hash size/encoding.
