## Description
Optimizes Redis hash operations by batching template mutations: instead of switching templates once per field during HSET/HDEL with multiple fields, all fields are collected first and a single template switch is performed per command. Reported improvement: ~2.7x-6x throughput depending on hash size/encoding. Files touched: `src/t_hash.c`, plus 3 test files (+719/-198 lines across 9 commits).
