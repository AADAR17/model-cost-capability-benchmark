## Description
Converts `IWorkbenchActionRegistry` into a simple forwarder rather than a data-holding entity — commands, keybindings, and menu registries already provide more flexible alternatives. Consolidates telemetry logging into the keybinding service and removes direct registry-based action lookups from the status bar.
