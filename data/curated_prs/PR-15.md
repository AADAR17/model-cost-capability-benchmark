## Description
Enhances Redis hash template test coverage and refactors the RDB save/load mechanism: replaces a mutable global "ref mode" variable with a flag-based approach (`RIO_FLAG_DUMP_PAYLOAD`) distinguishing RDB vs. DUMP-payload operations, and switches template-ID lookup during load from array indexing to a dictionary for clarity/robustness.
