## Description
Reduces dictionary lookups for RESTORE REPLACE on new keys: instead of find+delete+add (3 operations), reuses the link from the initial lookup during insertion — one lookup instead of three. Especially helpful during dictionary rehashing, where each lookup could trigger a separate incremental rehash step.
